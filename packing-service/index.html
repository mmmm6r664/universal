FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where does packing take place?
Either at our Box Store or at your home — whichever is more convenient, on a schedule that fits your move.
Do you pack fragile and special items?
Yes — fragile boxes, art and mirror framing, TV packing and crating, dish packs and wardrobe cartons are all available at the prices listed.
Can I pack some items myself?
Absolutely — pack what you like and we'll professionally pack the rest, ready for moving day.

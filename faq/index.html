Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my move?

For international moves we recommend booking 4–6 weeks ahead where possible. For local and interstate moves, 2–3 weeks is usually enough, but we accommodate shorter timelines whenever we can.

Do you provide packing materials and services?

Yes. We offer full and partial packing with export-grade materials, as well as specialty crating for fragile, high-value and oversized items.

How long does an international move take?

Transit time depends on origin, destination and shipping method. Ocean shipments typically take several weeks to a few months door-to-door; air freight is faster. Your move coordinator will give you an estimate for your specific route.

Is my shipment insured?

We offer transit insurance options so your belongings are protected throughout the journey. Ask your coordinator about all-risk coverage and valuation.

How do I get a quote?

Use our online Moving Calculator, book a free video pre-move survey, or submit the quote form and a move consultant will contact you.

Can I track my shipment?

Yes — we provide door-to-door tracking and keep you updated at every stage of your move.

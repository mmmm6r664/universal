Terms & Conditions

In these terms, "you" means the Customer and "we" means the Remover. The clauses below govern our quotation, each party's responsibilities, liability for loss or damage, and the handling of your goods.

1. Our Quotation

Quotes exclude customs duties and government fees, and may change for circumstances such as quotes not accepted within 28 days, currency or freight changes, weekend or out-of-hours work, and access problems.

2. Work Not Included

Excludes tasks such as dismantling flat-pack furniture, disconnecting appliances, lifting fitted floor coverings, and moving items from lofts unless access is safe.

3. Your Responsibility

You must declare goods' value in writing, obtain permits, be present or represented during collection and delivery, sign documents, protect goods left in unattended premises, and prepare appliances.

4. Our Responsibility

Goods should be delivered in the condition they were packed. Liability depends on the liability option chosen and on negligence or breach of contract.

5. Prohibited & Restricted Items

Hazardous items such as gas bottles are never moved. Valuables, perishables, animals and goods needing export licenses are excluded or require your own arrangements.

6. Ownership of Goods

You warrant that you own the goods or have authority from the owners, and will cover claims arising from a breach of this warranty.

7. Postponement or Cancellation Charges

Fees depend on notice given, ranging from no charge with more than 10 working days' notice to a higher percentage of the removal charge with short notice. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

8. Payment

Payment in cleared funds is due in advance, and no part of the agreed price may be withheld.

9. Liability Limits

Standard Liability (with a declared value and additional fee) and Limited Liability (a per-item cap) apply, with separate rules for international shipments.

10–12. Damage, Exclusions & Claim Time Limits

Liability for damage to property other than goods is limited to repairing the affected area, and must be noted on the paperwork or reported in writing within five days. Exclusions include fire, war and similar events under Limited Liability. Concealed damage must be reported within three days of delivery; written claims within nine months; and legal action within two years of a declined claim.

13–25. Further Terms

Additional clauses cover transit delays, our right to hold goods until charges are paid (lien), subcontracting, choice of route and method (including shared vehicle or container space), international removals advice, applicable law, forwarding address, inventories, storage-charge revisions, a war clause, sale or disposal of goods after non-payment, termination, and declined card authorizations.

For the complete, binding wording of any clause, please contact us.

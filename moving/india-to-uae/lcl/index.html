LCL Shipping from India to UAE

Less-than-Container-Load — share a container, pay only for the space you use.

Less-than-Container-Load — share a container, pay only for the space you use.

LCL (Less-than-Container-Load), also called groupage or shared-container shipping, is the most cost-effective way to move a part-load internationally. Your household goods are professionally packed, consolidated with other shipments into a shared ocean container, and separated again at destination for door delivery. You pay only for the volume (cubic feet / cubic metres) your goods occupy, which makes LCL ideal for studios, one-bedroom moves, and anyone shipping a modest load without the cost of a full container.

Pricing

What affects the cost of lcl shipping on this route

No two moves are the same — here's what shapes your quote, and why a free survey gives you a firm, honest price.

Volume or weight

The single biggest factor. Sea freight (LCL) is priced on the cubic feet/metres your goods occupy; FCL on the container size; air freight on chargeable weight. The more you move, the more it costs — which is why a pre-move survey and decluttering pay off.

Service level

Full-pack (we pack everything), partial-pack, or you-pack change the labour and materials. Specialist crating for art, pianos or marble adds cost but protects irreplaceable items.

Origin & destination access

Stairs, long carries, narrow streets, shuttle vehicles for large trucks, parking permits and high-floor deliveries all affect the quote. Tell us about access up front so there are no surprises.

Shipping mode & speed

Air is fastest and dearest; sea is slower and cheapest; a split shipment (a little by air, the rest by sea) balances the two.

Customs, duties & TR

Household goods often enter duty-free under Transfer of Residence rules if you qualify, but new items, vehicles and certain goods can attract duty. We confirm what applies before you ship.

Insurance & extras

All-risk transit insurance, storage, handyman services and vehicle or pet shipping are optional add-ons priced on what you choose.

Documents & customs

We prepare and lodge the paperwork, but having these ready keeps your move on schedule. Your coordinator sends an exact checklist for your route, including any Transfer of Residence or duty-relief you qualify for.

Valid passport (and visa or residence permit for the destination)
A detailed packing inventory / valued inventory for insurance
Transfer of Residence (TR) / returning-resident paperwork where you qualify
Proof of residence at origin and destination (lease, utility bill or similar)
For vehicles: title, registration and purchase documents
For pets: microchip, vaccination record, health certificate and import permit

What you can ship Allowed

  • Household furniture, appliances and white goods
  • Clothing, linens, books and personal effects
  • Kitchenware, crockery and cookware
  • Electronics and home-entertainment equipment
  • Children’s furniture, toys and sporting goods
  • Tools, bicycles and hobby equipment
  • Artwork, antiques and instruments (with specialist packing)
  • Vehicles (as a separate or combined shipment)

What not to ship Prohibited / restricted

  • Cash, jewellery, important documents and other irreplaceables (carry these yourself)
  • Flammables, aerosols, gas cylinders, paints and solvents
  • Firearms, ammunition and explosives
  • Perishable food and open liquids
  • Plants, seeds and some foodstuffs (destination quarantine rules vary)
  • Illegal or restricted substances of any kind

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does lcl shipping (groupage) from India to UAE take?
Transit time depends on the exact origin and destination and the season. Your dedicated move manager confirms a firm timeline for your India–UAE shipment after your survey. Air options are the fastest; ocean options are the most economical.
How is my India to UAE move priced?
LCL and baggage are priced on volume (or number of boxes), FCL on the container size you reserve, air freight on chargeable weight, and vehicle/pet moves per item with the required documentation. You get a firm, itemised quote after a free survey — no hidden fees.
Do you handle customs clearance in UAE?
Yes. We prepare all export paperwork in India and clear customs in UAE, including any Transfer of Residence or duty-relief paperwork you qualify for. Our in-house brokerage manages both ends so you don't deal with agents yourself.
What documents will I need?
Typically a passport, visa/residence proof, a detailed packing inventory and (for returning residents) Transfer of Residence paperwork. For vehicles you'll also need title and registration; for pets, vaccination and health records. We send you an exact checklist for your route.
Is my shipment insured?
Yes — we offer all-risk marine transit insurance. Your coordinator explains valuation and coverage so your belongings are protected door-to-door.
Can I get a quote without an in-home visit?
Absolutely. Book a free video survey from your phone, use our online Moving Calculator to estimate volume, or send us a room-by-room list and we'll quote from that.
Will you disassemble and reassemble my furniture?
Yes — our crews dismantle beds, wardrobes, tables and other large items for transit and reassemble them at delivery as part of a full-service move.
Can I pack some things myself?
Of course. Owner-packed cartons are fine; note that insurance on self-packed boxes may differ from professionally packed items, which your coordinator will explain.
What happens on moving day?
Our team arrives, protects floors and doorways, packs and wraps room by room, labels and inventories every item, and loads carefully. You get a copy of the inventory before we leave.
Do you offer storage if my dates don’t line up?
Yes — secure, climate-controlled storage for a few days or several months, with your inventory tracked and redelivery whenever you’re ready.
How do you protect fragile and valuable items?
Export-grade materials, double-wall cartons, custom crating for art and high-value pieces, and all-risk insurance so you’re covered door-to-door.

What we ship

Shipping options from Los Angeles to Gurgaon

LCL, FCL, baggage, pet or auto shipping — we tailor the method to your load, budget and timeline.

LCL shipment (shared container)

Moving a studio or one-bedroom load from Los Angeles to Gurgaon? LCL — less-than-container-load, also called groupage — is the most economical way to ship. Your goods are professionally packed, consolidated with other shipments into a shared ocean container, and you pay only for the volume you use. Ideal for partial loads and budget-conscious moves.

FCL shipment (full container)

For a two-bedroom home or larger from Los Angeles to Gurgaon, FCL gives you sole use of a 20ft or 40ft container. It’s loaded and sealed at your door, travels without being shared or re-handled, and is the fastest, most secure and most private way to ship a full home — and the best value once your volume is large enough.

Baggage & box shipping

Just sending a few boxes or suitcases from Los Angeles to Gurgaon? Our baggage shipping service collects from your door and delivers to your destination with transparent per-box pricing and no airline excess-baggage fees — by air when you need it fast, or by sea for the lowest cost. Perfect for students and single movers.

Pet shipping

Moving a pet from Los Angeles to Gurgaon? Our IPATA-affiliated pet relocation team handles airline-approved crates, bookings, vaccinations and import paperwork, with care at every step — door to doorstep, so your companion travels safely and comfortably.

Auto shipping

Shipping a car or motorcycle from Los Angeles to Gurgaon? We move vehicles by container or RoRo, handle all export and import formalities, and can load your vehicle alongside your household goods for a combined, cost-effective move.

What to know about moving from Los Angeles to Gurgaon

Most Los Angeles-to-Gurgaon household moves travel by sea freight, with air freight available for urgent or smaller shipments. We prepare all export and import documentation and clear customs in India, including any transfer-of-residence or duty relief you qualify for. Transit times vary by season and port; your dedicated move manager confirms exact timing and the most cost-effective option for your volume.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does moving from Los Angeles to Gurgaon cost?
It depends on your volume and the service — LCL (shared container), FCL (full container), baggage/boxes, pet or auto shipping. Use our Moving Calculator or book a free survey for a firm, itemised Los Angeles-to-Gurgaon quote with no hidden fees.
How long does shipping from Los Angeles to Gurgaon take?
Ocean freight from Los Angeles (the USA) to Gurgaon (India) is the economical option and takes several weeks door-to-door; air freight is far faster. Your move manager confirms exact timing after your survey.
Should I choose LCL or FCL from Los Angeles to Gurgaon?
LCL suits studios and one-bedroom loads — you pay only for the space you use. FCL suits two-bedroom homes and larger, giving you a private, faster, more secure container. We advise the right fit for your Los Angeles-to-Gurgaon move at survey.
Do you handle customs clearance in India?
Yes — we prepare all export paperwork in the USA and clear customs in India, including any Transfer of Residence or duty relief you qualify for.
Can you ship my car or pet too?
Yes — we ship vehicles (container or RoRo) and offer IPATA-affiliated pet relocation alongside your household goods from Los Angeles to Gurgaon.
